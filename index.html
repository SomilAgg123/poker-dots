WELCOME TO YOUR TABLE

A poker puzzle,
with a twist.

Draft one of three cards and place it anywhere on the 5 × 5 grid. The chosen card is replaced; the other two stay. All five rows and all five columns become poker hands.

  1. Build in two directions. Each card belongs to a row and a column. Only complete five-card hands score.
  2. Connect the three gold dots. Two pair or better in both crossing hands earns +12 per dot.
  3. Shape your luck. Refresh all three market cards up to three times. Swap two placed cards twice, even on a full board.
  4. Bank your table. Fill all 25 spaces, make final swaps, then bank. Aim for 100; reach 150 for Orbit.

Aces can be high or low in straights (A–2–3–4–5). No wraparound. Suits have equal value. Daily deals use the UTC date and are replayable; Undo reverses one action. Progress saves on this device when browser storage is available. No wagering or money.
